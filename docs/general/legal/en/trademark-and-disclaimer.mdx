## Brand

Use the LunaDeerMC name and Logo to identify the organization or its official work accurately. Do not recolor, distort, or redraw the Logo, and do not make third-party work look official.

## Disclaimer

Work and docs are provided as-is. Back up and verify compatibility before using a work on a production server; issues with external platforms or dependencies belong in their own channels.
