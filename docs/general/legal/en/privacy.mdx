## What we collect

The site is primarily static and does not require an account. Public channels such as GitHub, community spaces, and email handle information you choose to submit under their own policies.

## Third-party services

Work acquisition, code hosting, and external communities may open on third-party platforms. Read their privacy notices before submitting information there.
