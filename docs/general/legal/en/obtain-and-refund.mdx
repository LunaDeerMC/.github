## Acquisition

Free work is usually available through GitHub Releases or the public channels listed on its work page. Paid acquisition happens on a third-party platform; the site does not store order or payment data.

## Refunds

Refund rules, time limits, and handling steps follow the third-party platform and the relevant license. The site can point you to the right place but does not process refunds.
